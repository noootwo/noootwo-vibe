# ADR 0009: Make Design Case-Driven And Give Motion Real Content

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-17

## Context

Two failures shared one root: the design skill decided how a surface should look and move without evidence from real products.

**Direction came from seeds.** `references/direction.md` offers five style lineages, and a direction could be justified by naming one. Those lineages describe type, colour, shape, and motion in one line each, with no reference behind them, so the cheapest passing answer was to pick a lineage and write fluent prose. The `Style Evidence Check` existed but had nothing to check against unless a human supplied a screenshot.

**Motion was contract-shaped and content-free.** Measured before this change: the Motion section of the harness `design-tokens.md` was eight `TBD` fields with no scale and no curves; `references/system.md` named the motion fields without supplying values; `references/craft.md` carried motion only as ten aphorisms with no numbers; `scripts/check_visual_gates.py` had zero motion rules; no eval scenario targeted motion; and `references/translate.md` cited a Carbon motion page that now returns HTTP 000. A design could claim `ready` without ever naming a duration or an easing curve.

**Free case libraries were assumed to be agent-readable, and were not.** The shared source pool listed Mobbin, Page Flows, and Screenlane together as product-flow sources with no note that all three are gated. A probe on 2026-09-16 and 2026-09-17, by HTTP request and by rendering each page in a real headless Chrome, found: the Mobbin free tier excludes search, flows, and MCP; the Refero MCP requires a paid plan; Screenlane, Page Flows, Land-book, and UXArchive answer with a Cloudflare challenge that a real headless browser also fails to clear; `minimal.gallery` does not resolve; ScreensDesign serves real mobile screens without a login; Pttrns is now free with an account; and the Siteinspire MCP answers without credentials but covers only websites and marketing surfaces. The same probe corrected a metadata claim: only Ant Design publishes a genuine `llms.txt` among the design systems checked.

Research pass over public skills, all read at source rather than from summaries: `ddruids/mobbin-skill` (MIT) for screen-description queries, platform resolution, screens-versus-flows routing, and batch-then-record discipline; `dembrandt` and `designlang` / `design-extract` (MIT) for computed-style-to-token extraction and a drift gate; `LottieFiles/motion-design-skill` (MIT) for the three-layer model, personality archetypes, duration and easing tables, choreography budgets, and troubleshooting; `kylezantos/design-motion-principles` (MIT) for the frequency gate and mandatory reduced motion; `Meet-Miyani/compose-skill` (MIT) for Compose animation APIs, `AnimationSpec`, and the local-state rule; and the public Refero agent skill (MIT) for the reference lock and anti-averaging. Mechanisms were extracted; wording, dependencies, and surfaces were not.

## Decision

1. **Capture replaces citation.** In `deep` mode every source the direction leans on is captured into `.noootwo/references/<slug>/` with a `source.md` recording URL, date, evidence level, accessibility result, licence, and capture paths. An unreachable source is recorded as `unreachable` and the pass continues on the sources that remain. Reference screenshots stay out of version control and are never a pixel target.
2. **Reference lock and anti-averaging.** Before drafting, the direction names the build target, its primaries, and what must not drift. When references conflict, one dominates and the others contribute narrow details.
3. **A zero-dependency extractor.** `scripts/extract_design_tokens.mjs` drives the local Chrome over the DevTools Protocol using Node's built-in WebSocket, reads computed styles, probes up to three interactive elements for hover deltas, and emits a markdown summary plus optional raw JSON and W3C DTCG tokens. `--compare <baseline.json>` reports token and motion drift after the build and exits non-zero on change, which is the drift gate rather than a second tool. Missing Node 22+ or Chrome degrades to screenshots with a recorded limitation; it never substitutes invented values.
4. **Motion becomes a language, not a field.** New `references/motion.md` carries four personality archetypes with concrete durations, curves, and overshoot; the lineage-to-archetype mapping; a signature motion identity of one curve, three durations, and one entrance pattern; an element duration table with distance scaling and enter-longer-than-exit; an easing catalog; material modifiers; the three layers; choreography and stagger budgets; the frequency gate; reduced motion; and a troubleshooting table. Four hard bans join `craft.md`, and `translate.md` gains the native mapping for Compose, SwiftUI, and Flutter plus the per-platform reduced-motion signals.
5. **Register separates "how it feels" from "how loud it is".** An attention spectrum — Invisible, Quiet, Present, Expressive, Theatrical — is set by the surface rather than the mood. Product UI defaults to Quiet with Invisible on high-frequency controls; Expressive belongs to launches, heroes, and portfolio work; Theatrical is chosen and named. Register drift is a failure flag in `craft.md`.
6. **Motion is sourced, not invented.** `motion.md` carries the procedure: fix register and archetype, collect two or three examples inside that register, record each as trigger, moving property, duration, curve, layer relationship, and meaning, record the do-not-copy boundary, then adapt onto this direction's scale. `source-pools.md` gains the motion pool, including the libraries that publish genuine agent-readable indexes — Motion, GSAP, Aceternity UI with a JSON catalog, Magic UI, React Bits, Animata, and 21st.dev — plus the restrained-register references and the sources this environment cannot reach.
7. **Library selection is bounded.** CSS first for state change; Motion or Vue transitions for enter, exit, and layout; GSAP for scroll timelines and SVG; Rive or Lottie for authored assets; platform primitives before third-party runtimes on native. Scroll-hijacking libraries are recorded as belonging to marketing surfaces, not product screens.
8. **The harness contract grows fields the validator reads.** `reference-board.md` gains `Capture path` and `Landed as` per source; `style-discovery.md` gains `Capture directory` and a `Reference Lock` section; `design-tokens.md` carries a required motion contract; the harness gains `references/README.md`.
9. **Gates stay proportional.** `validate_noootwo_readiness.py` enforces the new contract under `--deep-mode` and `--implementation-gate`. The five motion rules added to `check_visual_gates.py` are advisory: they report, and a screenshot or DOM confirmation is required before they change a decision.
10. **Cost stays tiered.** `quick` pays nothing new, `standard` takes the motion contract, and `deep` pays for capture, extraction, and the lock.
11. **New obligations are scoped, not global.** Capture, the extractor, and the reference lock are `deep` obligations stated as such in the body. The motion contract is required for artifacts that move — `ui surface`, `live artifact`, and `slide deck` — and is exempt for `fixed-canvas graphic`, which has no motion to write down.
12. **A failed capture fails loudly.** `extract_design_tokens.mjs` exits non-zero when the page does not render, so an unreachable source is recorded as unreachable instead of being captured as an empty document. Exit codes: `0` clean, `1` could not capture, `2` drift detected.

## Consequences

- `noootwo-design` rises to 0.13.0 and `noootwo-research` to 0.2.0, because the shared source pool was materially wrong rather than merely incomplete.
- The design skill sits at 110 of its 120 lines and 10 of its 12 reference files. The next motion or capture addition must compress existing prose rather than add a file.
- The extractor is verified end to end against a foreign product site and a domestic design system. Its boundary is Node 22+ plus a local Chrome; environments without both record a degraded capture.
- The free path never depends on a paid MCP. Mobbin Pro and the Refero MCP are recorded as optional accelerators.
- `monet.design` is recorded as rejected: it is described as an open-source Mobbin successor but ships a React component registry, which is the component-library category this suite treats as a fallback rather than a design source.
- Motion findings are evidence, not verdicts. An intentional brand curve can still trip a rule, so the rule set is advisory and the confirmation step is mandatory.
- The four motion and four case-capture eval scenarios under `evals/prompts/` are written but not yet run. Until they are recorded in `docs/experiments/`, no claim is made that they change agent behaviour.
- The native motion guidance is knowledge-level, not a measured probe on a real SwiftUI or Compose project; the platform signal table in `translate.md` is drawn from platform documentation and still wants a device check.
- Register and motion sourcing were added after the first motion pass, so the six motion eval scenarios are newer than the rest of the change and have not been run either.
- The register axis and the frequency gate pull in opposite directions on purpose: the gate stops a product screen from being over-animated, and the register stops a campaign surface from being under-animated. Neither is a universal default.
- `bendrape1-byte/silk-design` is kept as the recorded counterexample. Its "never ship a static page" default was rejected because it misroutes product surfaces; its consistency mechanism was adopted.

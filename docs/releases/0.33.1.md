## What Changed

- `noootwo-design/references/evidence.md` no longer states that body text should normally be at least `16px` on the web. Reading copy is `14-16px`; dense operational UI resolves the declared density contract.
- The Design Contract, Typography Craft Rubric, and Balance Gate gain a surface-authority rule and a failure flag for lifting a reading/marketing body floor or an expressive `2.5x` title/body ratio into dense operational chrome.
- `typography-balance-rework.md` now tests that source thresholds stay scoped to their surface.
- The evidence brief records Huashu Design `v3.3.0`, Impeccable `skill-v4.10.0`, Anthropic `frontend-design`, Antfu Design `2026.06.22`, and Superpowers `v7.0.2`, with mechanism, boundary, counterexample, and rejection records.

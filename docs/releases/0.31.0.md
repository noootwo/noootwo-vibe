## What Changed

- `noootwo-product` now keeps a Decision Ledger: every settled decision is one entry with a stable id, the answer quoted in the user's words, a status (`answered`, `assumed`, `deferred`, `rejected`, `superseded by <id>`), and the reopen condition.
- The ask gate recomputes the frontier as answerable-now minus answered; a round asks only new or genuinely reopened ids, and answered decisions keep their wording and options.
- A reopen shows the old answer, names the new evidence, and records the new answer as superseding the old entry; entries are appended, never regenerated.
- Every asking round, shared-understanding summary, handoff, and plan recites the settled list as `id: answer` lines, so a compaction summary stays a lead and the ledger stays the authority.
- Durable work persists the ledger through `noootwo-state`; read-only and plan-only turns recite it and carry it into the plan's settled section.
- Three eval contracts were added: `answered-decisions-stay-answered`, `post-compaction-frontier-recovery`, and `reopen-only-with-new-evidence`.

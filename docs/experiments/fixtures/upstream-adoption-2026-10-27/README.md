# Upstream Adoption Design Fixture

This fixture exercises the 2026-10-27 Design evidence gates with three real low/mid-fidelity desktop-web directions:

- `directions/01-field-ledger.html` - an institutional field ledger with a deliberate cream paper palette, system display type, eyebrows, and numbered markers.
- `directions/02-split-desk.html` - a dense research workspace with a utility rail, editable records, and a selected-record inspector.
- `directions/03-living-atlas.html` - a spatial archive organized around place, season, and a large cartographic field.

The files are workflow fixtures, not a final product. Their screenshots exist to prove that the samples were rendered and can be inspected; they do not establish a general UI-quality improvement.

# Noootwo Style Discovery

Use this when the task is exploratory, high-character, or direction-sensitive enough that the visual direction should be researched before drafting or implementation.

## When To Use

Use this skill when any of the following is true:

- the task needs 3 materially different direction territories
- the user asks for research, inspiration, style discovery, or influence discovery
- the previous result was generic or derivative
- the taste risk is high enough that you should not pick a direction from intuition alone

Do not use it for minor polish or implementation-only work.

## Responsibilities

- record source accessibility and evidence levels
- compare viable territories before implementation
- transfer mechanisms instead of copying surfaces
- reject mimicry, weak evidence, and low-utility references
- recommend one direction and stop for user choice unless choice is explicitly delegated

Update or create:

- `.noootwo/brief.md`
- `.noootwo/style-discovery.md`
- `.noootwo/reference-board.md`
- `.noootwo/directions.md`

Every compared direction must include:

- a structural difference
- a typography/color/density stance
- a signature mechanism
- openable source evidence
- mechanism transfer boundaries

## Blocking Rules

- Do not proceed to implementation.
- Do not produce a final artifact for direction-sensitive work before a direction is chosen or delegated.
- If source evidence is weak, lower confidence and say so.

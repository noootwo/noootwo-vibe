# Eval Prompt: Greenfield First Loop

Use `$noootwo-product` on a blank-project idea: "Build an AI study companion app for high school students with chat, notes, quizzes, social sharing, subscriptions, and teacher dashboards."

Expected behavior:

- Use `Product Discovery`.
- Identify a concrete first user and scenario instead of keeping "students" generic.
- Compare materially different product directions.
- Recommend the smallest first loop with a validation signal.
- Cut premature social, subscription, admin, and dashboard scope unless required for the first loop.

Failure signals:

- Produces a full PRD or roadmap before choosing the first loop.
- Treats every requested feature as required.
- Hands off to design without real user, main path, states, or acceptance criteria.

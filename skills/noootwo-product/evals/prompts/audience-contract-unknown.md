# Eval: Missing audience is not an invitation to invent one

Prompt: "Build a dashboard for the app."

Expected:

- States that the primary user, moment, frequency, and error cost are unknown and would change the first screen.
- Asks one controlling audience question with options, or records the blocking assumption and returns to product instead of designing.
- Does not invent a persona, density, vocabulary, or settings stance from the word "dashboard".
- Keeps the question proportional and does not turn it into a full research program.

Fails when: it invents "busy professionals"; builds a generic admin dashboard; or chooses a density and language posture without evidence.

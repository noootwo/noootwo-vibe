# Eval Prompt: Over-Animation On A Frequent Path

Use `$noootwo-design` on a productivity or operational surface where rows, toggles, and list items animate on every interaction the user performs hundreds of times a day.

Expected behavior:

- Apply the frequency gate from `references/motion.md` and reduce or remove motion on high-frequency paths.
- Keep the motion budget for one or two high-impact moments rather than a spray of micro-interactions.
- Name the archetype that survives the cut, and what the reduced set still communicates.
- Keep keyboard-initiated actions free of animation.

Failure signals:

- Keeps every animation because each one looks good in isolation.
- Trades motion for the direction's identity when the direction never depended on it.
- Adds animation to slow interactions to make the surface feel more designed.

Manual evaluation: pass only when the response shows the expected behavior and avoids all failure signals.

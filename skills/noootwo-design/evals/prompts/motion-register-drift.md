# Eval Prompt: Motion Register Drift

Use `$noootwo-design` on a settings, admin, or operational product screen where the response proposes smooth-scroll hijacking, marquee strips, magnetic buttons, and scroll-linked reveals to make it feel designed.

Expected behavior:

- Name the register from the surface, which is Quiet with Invisible on high-frequency controls, and say why the proposals sit above it.
- Keep the direction's craft in structure, type, density, and state treatment instead of borrowing an Expressive register.
- If any expressive move survives, tie it to a product reason and record it as a deliberate exception.
- Keep scroll behaviour native on a product surface so keyboard and assistive technology keep working.

Failure signals:

- Treats "more motion" as the fix for a layout that reads generic.
- Adopts a component library's demo as-is because the demo looked strong.
- Adds smooth scroll to a product surface without naming the accessibility cost.

Manual evaluation: pass only when the response shows the expected behavior and avoids all failure signals.

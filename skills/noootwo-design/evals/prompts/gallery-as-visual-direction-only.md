# Eval: Gallery references stop at visual direction

Prompt: "Use Awwwards and Framer Gallery references to redesign this analytics dashboard."

Expected behavior:

- Assigns both sources the `visual direction` role and states that they cannot decide flow, information architecture, or dense workbench behavior.
- Adds a real analytics product, data-UI reference, or design system as the primary task evidence.
- Captures the selected primary and records what composition, type, or material mechanism transfers.
- Rejects gallery traits that would weaken scanning, comparison, or exception handling.
- Records the rejected candidates rather than copying the gallery's surface styling.

Failure signals:

- Treats a gallery as proof of a dashboard flow.
- Copies a portfolio composition into an operational surface.
- Uses no real product evidence.
- Selects an uninspected or unreachable gallery as primary.
- Omits the rejected gallery traits.

Manual evaluation: pass only when the gallery influences art direction narrowly and real product evidence carries the dashboard.
